Colour decisions:
- IMG_2851 is HDR (HLG). Standard tone-mapping turned the ivory to peach, so it was read without tone-mapping plus +10% contrast. Checked by eye against daylight photo 54e03c0a.
- Indoor clips (2845, 2846) have a warm room-light cast; a small red-down / blue-up colour balance was applied. No other colour changes.
- Not used: IMG_2844 (hands and loose threads in most frames), IMG_2847/2850 (strong colour cast, duplicates of 2845).
